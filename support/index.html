Support

需要帮忙？

Need a hand?

先看看下面几个常见问题。如果仍然没有解决，请给我们发邮件，我们会尽快回复。

Start with these common fixes. If the issue continues, email us and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.

照片无法导入

Photos won’t import

确认所选照片在系统授权范围内。如果原图保存在 iCloud，请保持网络连接，等待系统完成下载后再试。

Make sure the selected photos are included in your Photos permission. For iCloud originals, stay connected and let the system finish downloading them before trying again.

无法保存到相册

Can’t save to Photos

前往“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 拼图桌”，允许添加照片或完整访问，然后重新保存。

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Collage Desk, allow photo access, then try saving again.

桌面会保存在哪里？

Where is my desk saved?

拼图工程保存在当前设备上的应用数据中。删除 App 会同时删除这些工程；请先把需要的成品保存到系统相册。

Collage projects live in the app’s local data on this device. Deleting the app also deletes those projects, so save finished images to Photos first.

打赏或恢复购买

Tips and restoring purchases

打赏由 App Store 处理。如果状态没有更新，请确认使用原 Apple 账户，然后在“关于拼图桌”中选择恢复购买。

Tips are handled by the App Store. If the status hasn’t updated, use the original Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in About Collage Desk.

还是没有解决？

Still stuck?

请在邮件中附上设备型号、iOS 版本、问题描述和相关截图。

Include your device model, iOS version, a description of the problem, and any helpful screenshots.
