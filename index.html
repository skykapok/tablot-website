我喜欢摄影，每次把照片发到社交媒体前，都要先拼图。
以前用过的拼图 App，每拼一张都要重新去相册选照片，拼了几张之后，就记不清哪些已经用过了。

I love photography, and I make collages before sharing photos on social media.
With the collage apps I used, I had to pick photos from my library again for every collage. After a few, I couldn’t remember which ones I’d already used.

所以我做了拼图桌：把这次要发的照片一次选好，在一张小桌上拖拽拼接。没有花里胡哨的模板，没有广告，完全免费。希望也能帮到你。

So I made Collage Desk: choose all the photos for a post once, then drag them together on one little desk. No flashy templates, no ads, and completely free. I hope it helps you too.
