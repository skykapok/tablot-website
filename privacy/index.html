隐私政策

Privacy Policy

拼图桌以本地处理为原则。我们不运营用于上传照片或保存用户资料的服务器。

Collage Desk is designed around on-device processing. We do not operate servers for uploading photos or storing user profiles.

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 13 日Effective September 13, 2026

我们不收集的数据

拼图桌不要求注册账号，不包含广告、第三方统计工具或跨应用追踪，也不会收集姓名、联系方式、精确位置或设备标识符。

照片与拼图工程

你选择的照片仅用于在设备上创建和导出拼图。照片不会上传给开发者。拼图工程、布局和编辑信息保存在设备上的应用数据中。

如果照片原图位于 iCloud，Apple 的系统照片服务可能会按你的系统设置将其下载到设备。该过程由 Apple 提供，不会经过开发者服务器。

照片权限

拼图桌仅在你主动选择照片或保存拼图时请求系统照片权限。你可以随时在系统设置中更改授权范围。

App Store 购买

可选打赏由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和 App Store 处理。拼图桌不会接收或存储你的付款卡信息。购买状态用于在设备上确认打赏是否完成。

产品网站

本网站不包含自建分析、广告或追踪脚本。托管服务商可能按照其自身政策处理提供网页所必需的基础请求日志。

政策变更

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及 App Store 中的隐私信息，并在页面上注明新的生效日期。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 skykapok@gmail.com。

Data we do not collect

Collage Desk does not require an account and contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not collect names, contact details, precise location, or device identifiers.

Photos and collage projects

Photos you select are used only to create and export collages on your device. They are not uploaded to the developer. Collage projects, layouts, and editing information are stored in the app’s local data on your device.

If an original photo is stored in iCloud, Apple’s system Photos service may download it according to your system settings. This is provided by Apple and does not pass through developer-operated servers.

Photos permission

Collage Desk requests system Photos permission only when you choose photos or save a collage. You can change the permission at any time in Settings.

App Store purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Collage Desk does not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase status is used on device to confirm whether a tip was completed.

Product website

This website contains no custom analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process basic request logs needed to deliver the site under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and the privacy information in the App Store, with a new effective date shown here.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email skykapok@gmail.com.
